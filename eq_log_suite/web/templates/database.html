Rebuild

Re-reads the ticked logs from the start with the current parser and replaces all knowledge and history with what they give -- after a parser fix or a new fact, or to drop logs you don't want counted. It's built on a copy off to the side and swapped in only once it's finished, so if anything goes wrong (or a file is missing) nothing changes. Recording pauses meanwhile and catches up after; the Live view keeps working. Kept: alert rules, NPC/zone notes and overrides, rotation settings, log folders and the picker -- unless you reset settings too.
